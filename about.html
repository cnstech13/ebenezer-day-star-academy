WHO WE ARE

A School Built for Excellence

Ebenezer Day Star Academy is a modern educational institution committed to developing academically capable, confident and responsible young people.

We believe every child has unique abilities. Our responsibility is to create an environment where those abilities can be discovered, developed and transformed into meaningful achievements.

Through quality teaching, technology, extracurricular activities and character development, we prepare students for the future.
